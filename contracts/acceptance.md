# Acceptance

A completed experiment leaves `runs/<session-id>/` containing:

- `session.yaml`
- `evidence/` and `evidence/index.yaml`
- audit and hash manifests
- `analysis/summary.md`
- `verification/result.md`
- `research-report/report.md`
- `research-report/report.yaml`
- preservation and observability records

The report identifies authorization and scope, exact workload, environment and versions, instrumentation coverage, observations, inference, verification status, failures, limitations and reproducibility references.

Configuration alone is never PASS; it is NOT_DEPLOYED until runtime evidence exists.
