# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Cusimanse is an open-source security research project. We welcome contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Participants are expected to communicate professionally, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on improving the project and protecting researchers and users.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and technically specific.
- Welcome questions, corrections and alternative approaches.
- Give credit to contributors and upstream projects.
- Keep security research within authorized and legal boundaries.
- Protect secrets, personal information and sensitive forensic data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing credentials, private keys, personal data or sensitive incident evidence.
- Using project discussions or infrastructure to facilitate unauthorized access or abuse.
- Deliberately misleading contributors about security, testing or research results.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove or close contributions that violate this code of conduct and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the community safe.

For security vulnerabilities, do not use public issues. Follow `SECURITY.md` and use GitHub's private security reporting mechanism.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and interactions where participants are representing or participating in the Cusimanse community.
